## Evidence and claim levels

Source provenance (`authorityTier`) is separate from observation (`evidenceMode`). A genuine manufacturer rule recorded with a section identifier can support an operator behavior assertion. A photo documents relative layout but **not timing**. An unaudited video's title/description does not prove any press sequence or timestamp. Currently PUB-FCV-007/008 have **zero verified frames** and cannot establish a behavioral PASS. Public installation information is not proof of the classroom unit's firmware, transducer or options.

The `evaluateFcvPublicClaim` gate requires a source- and rights-backed FCV_OPERATOR trace row, actual behavior tests, completed browser QA and independent lesson review. Do not automatically promote the 64 drafted operator rows just because they appear in the inventory; their initial decisions remain `HOLD_UNTIL_IMPLEMENTED_AND_REVIEWED`. D15 is INSTRUCTOR_PLATFORM and never counted among the 64 FCV fidelity approvals. Synthetic transducer profiles with `firmwareInstalled=null` and `hardwareValidated=false` may support **training** without claiming physical equivalence.
